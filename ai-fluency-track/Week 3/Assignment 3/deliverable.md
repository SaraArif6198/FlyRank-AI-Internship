### Case order inside “Work”

1. **Inventory Management System** — the closest supporting match for an operations manager: POS, low-stock alerts, audit trail, reports, roles.
2. **The AI Core** — current internship engineering proof: explicit validation, provider independence, and 51 tests. Label it internship work, not client work.
3. **Customer Trends ETL** — shows auditable cleaning and analysis without inventing a business result.
4. **Mobile Expense Tracker** — a small honest adoption result: fully offline and still used by its one real user.
5. **Global Sales Dashboard** — interactive filtering and forecasting on public data.
6. **AURA** — LangGraph depth, thread isolation, and provider fallbacks; local/GitHub, one user.
7. **DataSage** — three-node EDA workflow and structured error states; tested on public datasets.

The order is deliberate: paid field proof first, then operations relevance, then engineering depth. AI work supports the claim; it does not replace it.
