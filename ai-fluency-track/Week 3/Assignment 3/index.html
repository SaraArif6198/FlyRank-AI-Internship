05 · AI options, human choice

Ten options. One survived.

AI widened the field. Sara chose the line that was specific, defensible, and connected to the strongest case.

  1. I build business software that works where the work happens.
  2. I replace manual business processes with software teams can rely on.
  3. I turn paper workflows into production systems.
  4. I build offline-first tools for operations that cannot stop.
  5. I make fragile manual processes measurable, reliable, and digital.
  6. I build the software behind smoother day-to-day operations.
  7. I turn registers and spreadsheets into working business systems.
  8. I ship practical software for teams working beyond reliable internet.
  9. I build production systems for the processes businesses still run by hand.
  10. ChosenI build production software that replaces paper processes—even where the internet doesn’t reach.
Why this one: “Production” rules out demos. “Replaces paper processes” names the work. The final clause makes the real DG Cement constraint memorable without promoting AI work into the headline.
