# Week 3 — The Through-Line: Map Content & CTAs

My one-line claim:

> **I build production software that replaces paper processes—even where the internet doesn’t reach.**

I tested ten options, then kept the one that names both the work and the real constraint behind my strongest proof.

My portfolio will be one scrolling page:

1. **Hero** — claim and measured proof → *See the field proof*
2. **DG Cement proof** — paid, offline-first payroll and attendance system → *See the systems behind the claim*
3. **Selected work** — operations first, then data and AI depth → *See how I deliver*
4. **How I work** — understand, design for failure, test, hand over, support → *Scope your process*
5. **Scope builder** — turns the visitor’s current process into a useful brief → *Build my project inquiry*
6. **Contact** — editable scoped message → *Email me a project inquiry*

The CTA names change with the visitor’s readiness, but they all lead to the one action I chose in Week 1: an operations manager emails me a scoped project inquiry.

DG Cement leads. Inventory follows because it is the closest operations match. The AI Core and the other AI/data projects show technical depth; they do not replace the paid field proof.

Still to gather before publish: redacted DG Cement screens, confirmation of the DG Cement testimonial paraphrase, direct Inventory and AURA captures, public/private status for every repo and demo, a DataSage result-state capture, and exact dataset links where relevant. Missing proof stays marked missing until it is real.
