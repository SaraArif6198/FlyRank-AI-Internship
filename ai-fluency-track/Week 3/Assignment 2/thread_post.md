# Week 3 — Kill Your Darlings: Curate Your Images

My rule: if the image is evidence of my work, it must come from the work. If the image is me, it must come from a camera. AI may frame the story; it may not fabricate the proof.

**Keepers**

- One generated hero texture in my forest / ink / warm-paper / signal-lime identity system.
- Real UI, Swagger, and test captures for The AI Core.
- Real portfolio captures for Customer Trends, Expense Tracker, Global Superstore, and DataSage.
- A real photo of me for the contact moment.
- Real captures—not generated mockups—specified for DG Cement and every other project case.

I also chose **no image** for “How I work” and the scope builder. The copy and interaction already do the job.

**What I rejected:** I cut a second generated hero option even though it was polished and matched the palette. Its dense cards and crossing lines competed with the headline, read as generic “complex AI,” and made the portfolio look busier than the practical field software it is meant to frame.

I also cut most of the old portfolio asset dump: tech logos, certificates, unrelated projects, and glossy mockups do not become keepers just because they already exist.

Three safe real captures still remain: DG Cement, Inventory, and AURA. I marked those gaps instead of treating presentation mockups as product proof.
