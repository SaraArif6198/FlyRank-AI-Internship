## The rule

If the image is evidence of my work, it must come from the work. If the image is me, it must come from a camera. AI may frame the story; it may not fabricate the proof.

- **DG Cement:** actual attendance, offline state, and payroll screens. This is the paid proof; an AI mockup would make the strongest claim less believable.
- **Customer Trends, expense tracker, Global Sales, and DataSage:** selected from the newly added real portfolio assets because each maps to a current case and remains legible.
- **Inventory and AURA:** still require direct captures. The existing polished inventory desktop mockup and generic “Project Sync” chat image are not strong enough to count as proof of those builds.
- **Sara:** a real portrait. No AI “professional headshot.”
- **How I work and scope builder:** no image. The interaction and copy do the job; decorative icons would add noise.

The asset export also contains certificates, technology logos, social icons, older projects, and several glossy UI mockups. I excluded them because the sitemap does not need them or because they look more like presentation art than direct product evidence.

Three genuine capture gaps remain: DG Cement, Inventory, and AURA.
